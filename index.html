WHO IS NOIR?

Crafted for Those
Who Hold Steady Hands

NOIR is a modern black-and-white fine jewellery maison conceived exclusively for physicians, surgeons, residents, and medical professionals. Where traditional luxury houses design for stillness, NOIR designs for twelve-hour rounds, sterile suites, and quiet moments beyond the hospital doors.

Every link, bezel, and South Sea pearl mount is cast in non-reactive 950 platinum and carbonado black diamonds—balanced with low-profile galleries, smooth interior bevels, and enduring monochrome poise.
